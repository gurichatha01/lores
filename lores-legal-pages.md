PRIVACY POLICY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Last updated: [LAST UPDATED]

Lore ("lores.in", "we", "us") is a tool that turns an exported chat into a written report
about a relationship or group. Privacy is central to how the product is built. This policy
explains, plainly, what we do and do not handle.

## Your chat is processed on your device

When you upload an exported chat, the parsing and analysis happen entirely in your browser.
Your raw chat — the actual messages — is never uploaded to our servers and is never stored
by us. It stays on your device.

To write your report, we send a limited, derived payload to our AI provider: anonymised
statistics (counts, timings, and similar aggregates) and a small sample of messages needed
to make the writing specific. We do not retain this payload after your report is generated.

## What we store

- **Single reports (₹49):** No account is required and we store no personal information
  about you. The generated report is held in your browser, not in our database.
- **10-report packs (₹399):** To let you return and use your remaining reports, a pack
  creates an account. For that account we store your email address and the number of
  reports you have left. We do not store your chats or your generated reports.
- **Payments:** Payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive confirmation that a payment
  succeeded and a payment reference; we do not receive or store your full card details.
  Razorpay handles payment information under its own policies.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your chats for advertising.

## Cookies and sessions

For pack accounts, we use a login session (a cookie/token) so you stay signed in. We do not
use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will
update this policy.

## Deleting your account and data

If you have a pack account, you can delete it at any time from your account page. Deleting
your account removes your login and your remaining-reports record from our systems. You can
also contact us at chathags@outlook.com to request deletion.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: chathags@outlook.com

automated AI systems based on the data you upload. Because reports are AI-generated, they are
intended as entertainment and keepsakes, and may contain inaccuracies or interpretations you
disagree with. They are not statements of fact about any person.

## Your responsibilities

- You are responsible for how you share any report you generate.

## Digital goods, delivered instantly

Lore reports and pack credits are digital products delivered immediately. Because of this:

- **Reports and pack credits are non-refundable once delivered.** We do not offer refunds for
  change of mind, for dissatisfaction with AI-generated content, or for unused pack credits.

## The one exception — we failed to deliver

If you were charged but a technical failure on our side prevented your report from being
delivered or your pack credits from being applied, contact us at chathags@outlook.com with
your payment reference. We will either resolve the issue so you receive what you paid for, or
refund that specific payment.

## Cancellation

There are no recurring subscriptions to cancel. A pack is a one-time purchase of ten report
credits. You may delete your account at any time from your account page; deleting your account
does not create a refund for unused credits.
